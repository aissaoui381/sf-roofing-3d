**Q2**: Do you handle SF DBI permits?
**A**: Yes — every roof replacement we do is permitted through the San

**Q3**: What roofing materials work best in San Francisco?
**A**: For fog-belt neighborhoods like the Sunset and Richmond, we
recommend modified bitumen or TPO on flat roofs and Class A asphalt
shingles or metal on pitched roofs. Twin Peaks and Pacific Heights
hillside homes often suit Spanish tile.

**Q4**: Do you offer free estimates?

permits, and timeline — delivered by email with no commitment.

**Q5**: What neighborhoods do you serve?
**A**: All of San Francisco — Mission District, Twin Peaks, Sunset
District, Richmond District, SOMA, Pacific Heights, Noe Valley, and
every other neighborhood in the city.

## 13. Reviews — Outreach Plan

After the profile is live, generate a Google review request link:
1. Profile dashboard → "Get more reviews"
2. Copy the short link Google generates
3. Email past customers with: "If you were happy with our work, would you
   leave us a quick review? <link>"

For a Page-3 → Page-1 push in a competitive SF market, you'll want at
least 40-50 Google reviews with a 4.7+ average. Most local-SEO experts
treat **40 reviews** as the magic number where Google starts trusting
the listing as established.
