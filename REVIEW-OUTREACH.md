# Review Outreach — Email & SMS Templates

For asking past San Francisco Roofing Service customers to leave a Google
review. Goal: 40-50 Google reviews at a 4.7+ average within the first
6 months.

> **When to send**: 3-7 days after final invoice. Long enough for the
> homeowner to live with the new roof through one rain or wind event.
> Short enough that the experience is fresh.

---

## Template 1 — Initial Ask (Email)

**Subject line options** (pick whichever feels right; A/B test if you can):
- `Quick favor — would you leave us a review?`
- `How did we do on your {{neighborhood}} roof?`
- `One favor to ask, {{first_name}}`
- `Your roof is done — a quick favor`

**Body:**

```
Hi {{first_name}},

It was a pleasure working on your {{project_type}} at {{property_street}}.
We hope the new roof is holding up beautifully through the first
{{season}} weather.

Quick favor — if you were happy with our work, would you take 60 seconds
to leave us a Google review? It genuinely helps other San Francisco
homeowners find us when they need roofing help.

Here's the direct link:
{{google_review_link}}

If anything wasn't perfect, please reply to this email first — I'd
rather hear it from you and make it right than have you write about
it publicly.

Thanks so much,
{{owner_first_name}}
San Francisco Roofing Service
Contact@SanFranciscoRoofingService.com
```

**Placeholders to fill in:**
- `{{first_name}}` — customer first name
- `{{project_type}}` — "roof replacement" / "flat roof installation" / "leak repair"
- `{{property_street}}` — "your Noe Valley home" / "your Sunset flat"
- `{{season}}` — "winter" / "summer fog" / "fall windstorms"
- `{{google_review_link}}` — your short Google review link (see Setup section)
- `{{owner_first_name}}` — the name of whoever signed the original contract

---

## Template 2 — Follow-up (7-10 days later, only if no response)

**Subject**: `Quick reminder — {{first_name}}`

**Body:**

```
Hi {{first_name}},

Just a quick reminder in case my last email got buried — if you have
60 seconds, a Google review would mean a lot to our small SF team:

{{google_review_link}}

No worries at all if you're slammed. Thanks for trusting us with your
roof.

— {{owner_first_name}}
```

Send only **once**. Three asks crosses the line from polite to pushy
and you'll get flagged in spam.

---

## Template 3 — SMS Version (for younger / mobile-first customers)

```
Hi {{first_name}}, it's {{owner_first_name}} from San Francisco
Roofing Service. If you were happy with the {{project_type}}, would
you mind leaving us a quick Google review? Helps us a lot:
{{google_review_link}}
```

Keep under 320 characters (two SMS segments). Don't send if the
customer hasn't already received texts from you during the job.

---

## Template 4 — Reply When They DO Leave a Review

Reply to **every** review within 48 hours, positive or negative.
This is a strong ranking signal in GBP.

**For a 5-star review:**

```
Thanks so much, {{first_name}}! It was a pleasure working on your
{{project_type}} in {{neighborhood}}. If anything ever comes up with
the roof, you know where to find us.

— The San Francisco Roofing Service team
```

**For a 4-star review:**

```
Thanks for the honest review, {{first_name}}, and for trusting us
with your {{neighborhood}} roof. If there's anything specific we
could have done better, please email Contact@SanFranciscoRoofingService.com —
we'd genuinely like to know.

— {{owner_first_name}}
```

---

## Template 5 — Damage Control (1-3 Star Review)

Never argue publicly. Take it private.

```
{{first_name}}, thank you for taking the time to share this. I'm
sorry your experience didn't meet the standard we hold ourselves to.
I'd like to make it right — could you email me directly at
Contact@SanFranciscoRoofingService.com or reply here with the best
time to call? I'll personally look into what happened.

— {{owner_first_name}}, owner
```

After the issue is genuinely resolved offline, you can politely ask
the customer to update the review. Never offer money or anything of
value in exchange for a review — Google will catch it and ban the
listing.

---

## Setup — Generate the Google Review Link

Once your Google Business Profile is verified:

1. Open https://business.google.com
2. Dashboard → "Get more reviews" (or "Ask for reviews")
3. Google generates a short URL like `g.page/r/CXXXXXXXXX/review`
4. Copy that and paste it everywhere `{{google_review_link}}` appears

This short URL takes the customer directly to a 5-star prompt — no
searching, no navigating. Critical for SMS where character count matters.

## Cadence & Numbers

- **Days 1-3 after final invoice**: send Template 1
- **Day 10**: if no review yet, send Template 2 (one time only)
- **Day 14 onward**: stop emailing this customer about reviews

Expected response rate: **15-25%** of emailed customers leave a review.
So to hit 40 reviews you need to email 160-260 past customers. If your
project log doesn't have that many yet, mix in 4-6 reviews per month
from new jobs as they close.

---

## Don't

- Don't ask in-person at job completion ("Hey, could you review us?") —
  it's awkward and people forget. Email/SMS is better.
- Don't text and email the same customer about the same review request.
- Don't offer discounts or refunds in exchange for reviews (Google ToS
  violation — automatic delisting).
- Don't write fake reviews from inside the company. Google's machine
  learning catches this in days and bans the listing permanently.
- Don't only ask happy customers. The cherry-picking pattern is
  detectable and lowers trust signals.
